How this page calculates the result

A TCGA patient is scored by the models from the fold that held that patient out, so the result is the same out-of-fold prediction the reports use. The page computes it live from exported model parameters. For all 1,083 patients it matches the pipeline's saved Cox risk to within 0.000001.

A new patient is scored by averaging the five fold models. The GNN needs a trained graph network, so here its term is held at the cohort average. In the fused model that term carries almost no weight: fixing it for the TCGA patients gives a rank correlation of 0.99 with the full model and moves the median patient by 2 percentile points. The neural part of each path is the mean importance that path earned across the cohort.

Fields you do not set fall back to cohort medians. Everything runs in this browser, and nothing you enter leaves the page.

Trained on 1,083 TCGA BRCA patients with 145 recorded progressions. Fused model C-index 0.664 ± 0.021 (clinical Cox 0.675 ± 0.035, a statistical tie). Logic engine 0.628 ± 0.055, with 69% of its formulas using PrimeKG. The estimated month is within 12 months for 48% of held-out progressors.
